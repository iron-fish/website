Over the two years since mainnet launch, the Iron Fish blockchain has produced more than 1.3 million blocks and processed over 2.9 million transactions. Across five years, we’ve built a production‑grade privacy chain and a meaningful suite of tools—including privacy‑preserving USDC via the Chainport bridge. Yet because privacy often requires bridging, most users still rely on relatively centralized bridges, introducing security trade‑offs that keep many from exercising their basic right to transact privately.

Iron Fish remains steadfast in its founding mission: bringing privacy to every user and every blockchain. To that end, we’re launching Iron Fish v2. If users can’t come to us, we’ll meet them where they are.

Iron Fish v2 will deploy the Iron Fish privacy engine across additional chains, delivering native, chain‑level privacy to a broader set of users, while the Iron Fish blockchain continues to serve as the most capable privacy engine.

## SuiFish

### About Sui

Sui is a fast‑growing Layer‑1 with strong user adoption and expanding on‑chain liquidity.

- **Users**: Millions of active addresses and a vibrant retail user base.
- **On‑chain assets (TVL)**: ~$1.7B across DeFi protocols (per [DeFiLlama — Sui](https://defillama.com/chain/Sui)).
- **Activity**: High daily transaction volumes and rising cross‑chain inflows.

These dynamics make Sui a strong first venue for bringing the Iron Fish privacy engine to mainstream users.

### Planned SuiFish features

- **Shield**: Convert any Sui token into a SuiFish private asset.
- **Unshield**: Convert SuiFish private assets back into Sui tokens.
- **Private transfers**: Send SuiFish assets privately to other users.
- **Private DeFi**: Private swaps and lending.

## Privacy, continued

Global policy is converging on “privacy with accountability.” The EU is implementing MiCA alongside a new AML authority; FATF’s Travel Rule is being enforced across jurisdictions; the US is increasing oversight of mixers; and the UK, Hong Kong, and Singapore are rolling out comprehensive regimes. The upshot: compliant, selective‑disclosure privacy is becoming the standard for mainstream adoption.

Iron Fish continues advancing practical, compliant privacy for real users and real apps:

- **Broader deployments**: Roll out the Iron Fish privacy engine across additional L1/L2 ecosystems to meet users where they are.
- **Seamless UX**: Tighter wallet integrations, low‑friction shielding/unshielding, and clear privacy indicators by default.
- **Developer‑first**: SDKs, indexers, and reference apps that make building private flows—payments, swaps, lending—straightforward.
- **Decentralized infrastructure**: Permissionless relayers/provers, batching, and fee markets for reliability and lower costs.
- **Programmable compliance**: Opt‑in view keys and selective disclosure to satisfy audits without sacrificing user privacy.

Our goal is simple: make privacy the default—usable, affordable, and composable—on the chains people already use.
